## Caption (copy-paste)

Your data can be right AND lying to you at the same time. 📉📈

40 ad campaigns. Spend vs sales.
Overall, the trend says: spend MORE → sell LESS. 🤯

Split by region? Inside EVERY region, more spend → MORE sales.

That's Simpson's paradox: a hidden variable (region) drives both spend and sales, so the average points the wrong way. Trust the average and you'd cut the budget that was actually working.

Before you trust any trend, ask: what's hiding in the average?
