# Resume bullet drafts

**Read this first:** these describe a self-directed **portfolio project**
built on synthetic data — put them in a resume's **Projects** section, not
merged into your Experience bullets for Myntra. The specific numbers below
(73.8% growth, 8pt retention drop, etc.) came from simulated data and would
misrepresent real employer results if used as if they were Myntra's actual
numbers. What's real and yours to claim: the SQL, the statistics, the
experiment design, and the product-prioritization judgment. Edit the
framing/company-context language to match how you want to present it, but
don't swap in real Myntra figures unless they're actually true and you're
cleared to share them externally.

One line to adapt for a Projects-section header, whichever length fits:

> **Men's Footwear Growth & Product Portfolio** (personal project) —

## For a Manager, Analytics — style resume (execution-focused)

- Built an end-to-end category growth diagnostic (SQL + Python) on
  ~119K simulated e-commerce order records, decomposing revenue growth into
  demand, discounting, and retention effects, and identifying a specific
  cohort-retention drop-off as the largest addressable growth lever.
- Designed and analyzed an A/B test end to end — pre-registered hypothesis
  and power/sample-size calculation, two-proportion z-test and Welch's
  t-test for primary and guardrail metrics — producing a scoped rollout
  recommendation after a guardrail metric showed a real regression.
- Authored a product requirements document and a RICE-scored, 7-initiative
  roadmap, translating quantitative findings into a sequenced product plan.

## For a Senior Manager, Analytics — style resume (judgment/rigor-focused)

- Led a category growth diagnostic spanning demand, retention, and channel
  economics; identified that a naive discount-elasticity estimate was
  confounded by seasonal timing rather than reporting it as a causal
  effect — the kind of finding that changes a pricing recommendation instead
  of just describing a correlation.
- Owned the full experimentation lifecycle for a conversion-focused UX
  test: hypothesis, statistical power calculation, and a nuanced go/no-go
  call after a guardrail metric (return rate) showed a statistically
  significant regression alongside a primary-metric win — recommending a
  scoped mitigation over a blanket rollout.
- Built the statistical toolkit (hypothesis tests, power calculations) from
  first principles in an environment with no access to standard data
  science libraries, validating each implementation against known
  reference values before using it to drive a business recommendation.

## For a Product Management pivot — style resume

- Authored a full PRD (problem framing, phased scope, success metrics,
  risks/dependencies) for a size-and-fit recommendation feature, grounding
  the problem statement in a quantified return-rate gap from prior
  analytics work rather than an assumption.
- Built a RICE-scored roadmap across 7 competing initiatives computed
  programmatically from underlying inputs; explicitly identified and
  corrected for a known RICE limitation (it under-scores infrastructure
  work with no direct user reach) when sequencing a blocking dependency
  ahead of a higher-scored feature.
- Bridged an analytics background into product decisions: used a completed
  A/B test's actual result (not an estimate) to give one roadmap item the
  highest confidence score in the prioritization model, demonstrating how
  experimentation discipline should inform — not just follow — product
  prioritization.
